Equipment data and item thumbnails

This offline equipment snapshot was adapted from the equipment tables maintained
by the BG3 Wiki community at https://bg3.wiki/. The source field of each JSON
record links to its wiki article and contributor history. The importer combines
category tables, normalises fields for calculation, converts HTML descriptions
to plain text, and deduplicates thumbnails.

The “Where to find” snapshot uses the acquisition section of each linked item
article. Act labels use BG3 Wiki's act-specific location categories. For
supplementary routes, the card links to the wiki trader, loot or location page.
